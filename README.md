# edwin - The DeFAI Layer

## The Missing Bridge in DeFAI

The world of finance is experiencing two parallel revolutions. AI assistants like ChatGPT, Claude, and Siri have transformed how we interact with technology, enabling natural language conversations to complete complex tasks. Simultaneously, DeFi protocols like [Aave](https://aave.com/), [Uniswap](https://uniswap.org/), and [Morpho](https://morpho.xyz/) have built an open, programmable financial system managing billions in assets. Yet a critical gap exists - AI assistants excel at understanding user intent but lack native DeFi capabilities, while DeFi protocols remain isolated behind complex interfaces requiring specialized knowledge, creating a barrier for mainstream adoption.

#### What is edwin?

edwin connects your favorite AI assistants directly to DeFi protocols. By providing a secure bridge and handling blockchain operations invisibly, **edwin allows ChatGPT, Claude, Siri, and other AI interfaces to manage your investments, giving you early access to tomorrow's trading interface.**

### Architecture

edwin consists of several core components that work together to enable AI assistants to interact with DeFi:

#### Core Components

* **Model Context Protocol (MCP)**: Securely translates AI assistant instructions into on-chain actions
* **Edwin Cloud**: Managed infrastructure for seamless AI-to-DeFi connections
* **Security Layer**: Transaction verification, simulation, and policy enforcement

### Use Cases

edwin enables everyday users to access DeFi through familiar AI assistants. Here are just a few examples of what you can do:

* **One-sentence swaps**: "Trade half my ETH for ARB and stake the rest."
* **Passive Income Setup**: "Move my USDC to the highest yielding stable farm under $100 gas."
* **Portfolio Management**: "Rebalance my positions to 40% ETH, 30% BTC, 30% stables."
* **Automated Strategies**: "If RSI drops below 40, swap half my USDC to ETH."
* **Position Monitoring**: "Alert me when my loan-to-value ratio exceeds 65%."
* **Market Intelligence**: "Compare yield opportunities across Arbitrum and Base."
* **Gas Optimization**: "Swap ETH for USDC when gas is below 30 gwei."

The possibilities expand daily as AI assistants become more capable.
