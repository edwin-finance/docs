# Quickstart

This guide provides a quick and official way to integrate edwin with your Claude environment.

#### Prerequisites

To use edwin with Claude, ensure you have the following:

1. **Claude Max Plan:** edwin integration requires a subscription to the Claude Max plan.
2. **Compatible Cryptocurrency Wallet:** A web3-compatible cryptocurrency wallet (e.g., Phantom, MetaMask) is essential for interacting with decentralized finance protocols via edwin.

#### Integration Steps

Follow these steps to connect edwin to Claude:

1. **Access Claude Settings:** In Claude, click your profile icon (bottom left) and select "Settings."
2. **Navigate to Integrations:** Go to the "Integrations" section.
3. **Add Custom Integration:** Click "Add more" under existing integrations.
4. **Enter edwin Details:** In the "Custom integrations" window:
   * **Integration name:** `edwin`
   * **Integration URL:** `https://app.edwin.finance/sse`
   * Click "Add."
5. **Connect Wallet:** Locate "edwin" in your integrations list and click "Connect." A new tab will open, prompting you to connect your crypto wallet. Select your wallet and approve the connection request.
6. **Confirmation:** Upon successful connection, you'll see a confirmation message, and Claude will notify you with "Successfully connected to edwin server."

#### Using edwin with Claude

Once connected, you can leverage edwin's capabilities directly within Claude conversations:

1. **Initiate Query:** Ask Claude a question that requires edwin, such as "What's in my Solana wallet?"
2. **Grant Permission:** Claude will ask for permission to use the "edwin" integration. Select "Allow once" or "Allow always."
3. **Receive Response:** Claude will then retrieve and display the requested information using edwin.
